# Brand profile (one file per page)

Everything that belongs to ONE page lives here, never in the generic production references. Save it as
`.agents/brand-profile.md` (or `.claude/brand-profile.md`) next to the page's code, and read it before any
production step. The generic rules in this skill say *how* to make content; this file says *for whom, in what
voice, about what, and with which limits*.

Fill every section. Leave nothing implied: an automated run only knows what is written.

## 1. Identity
- Page handle, platform account id (for identity checks before publishing).
- Who is behind it and what they want to be seen as (e.g. "a consultant who builds AI automation for
  businesses" → covers must read as expert, never meme-like).
- Primary goal (follows / leads / traffic to a product / authority). Secondary goal.
- Product or site to send people to, if any; how often a call-to-action to it may appear, and where never
  (e.g. never on tragedy or safety topics); claims about it that are allowed and forbidden.

## 2. Audience
- Who, where, which language and register (spoken vs written), age range.
- Their real situation today: money, prices, currency, which services work or are blocked for them,
  internet conditions, legal limits, local risks. Every tip is checked against this.
- What they share with family and friends (the topics that already won on this page, with numbers).

## 3. Topic charter
- The page's one-line job (e.g. "the role of AI and tech in people's daily life, told so they share it").
- Topic test: what every topic must connect (e.g. daily life AND technology); what is out (pure news,
  pure social warnings, B2B deal news…).
- Standing topic families and their share of the week (e.g. 2 of 3 reels about the viewer's own
  job/money/family; at least 2 a week about the owner's own field).
- Formats the owner liked or banned, with the example that set the rule.

## 4. Voice and look
- Tone (calm, practical, true…), banned words and hype, emoji policy.
- Words never allowed on covers (the page's "fatigue word", e.g. «هوش مصنوعی» on an AI page).
- Brand colours (or "vary per post"), fonts, handle placement, footer rules.
- Model covers and model headlines the owner approved (paste them; they are the bar).

## 5. Cadence and slots
- Time zone; slots per day per format (e.g. reels 09:00 / 15:00 / 21:00, carousel every other day 12:00);
  publish window length; what happens to a late slot.
- Series days and slots.

## 6. Accounts and storage
- Where state, files and logs live (Drive folder ids, sheet id and tabs), connector account aliases.
- Voice library ids that suit this page; music and SFX libraries.
- Image model and the per-package image budget.

## 7. Owner's standing decisions (log)
Date · the owner's exact words · the rule it became. Newest first. Production reads this before every run;
a decision here beats a generic default in this skill.

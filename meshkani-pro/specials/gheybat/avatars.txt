Character design sheet for a comedy animated short. One wide 16:9 image divided into FIVE equal vertical panels side by side, separated by thin pure-black gutters. Stylised 3D animated-film look (premium, warm, expressive, like a modern feature animation still), the same cosy night café in every panel: warm wooden table, espresso cups, soft amber lamps, rain with a faint digital glitch shimmer on the window behind. Each panel shows ONE character sitting at the café table, seen from the waist up, facing the camera, with a normal human body, human hands and everyday clothes, but the HEAD is not human: it is an AI-agent device head with a face made only of simple glowing shapes (eyes and mouth as light), very expressive. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no brand marks anywhere.
Panel 1 — SARA, the office manager (woman): elegant dark blazer over a cream top, a thin gold bracelet, relaxed confident posture, holding a coffee cup. Head: a sleek rounded smoked-glass smart-display orb with a warm amber glowing face, clever half-smile, one raised eyebrow shape.
Panel 2 — NIMA, the operations agent (man): rumpled shirt with rolled sleeves and a loosened tie, slumped and exhausted, hands thrown up. Head: a graphics card (GPU) turned upright, two round cooling fans as eyes, glowing red-orange from overheating, thin wisps of smoke, a wobbly worried mouth of red light.
Panel 3 — AMIR, the technical agent (man): dark hoodie, arms crossed, dry deadpan attitude. Head: a small retro CRT terminal monitor with a green phosphor screen, flat unimpressed eyes drawn as two green bars and a straight line mouth.
Panel 4 — KAVEH, the creative agent (man): colourful scarf, paint-stained denim jacket, leaning forward excitedly with open hands. Head: a big glowing light bulb filled with swirling rainbow-gradient light, sparkling, wide happy eyes of light.
Panel 5 — NOVA, the calendar agent (woman): soft pastel cardigan, calm and kind, hands folded around a cup. Head: a rounded soft-white desk-clock and flip-calendar device, pastel mint glow, gentle closed-eye smile made of light, small clock hands on top like a hair clip.
Consistent art direction across all five so they clearly belong to one cast; each silhouette and head colour clearly different.

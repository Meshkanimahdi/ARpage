## Scale

- Hand uploads: 601,565 views, 579,811 of them one reel («همه می‌دونن…», another page's video).

## Distribution of the 44 pipeline reels
| views | reels | median retention | median avg watch | median shares / 1k reach |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ≥ 10k | 4 | 50 % | 27.9 s | 164 |
| 3–10k | 1 (REEL-054) | 37 % | 15.2 s | 160 |
| 1–3k | 32 | 29 % | 14.4 s | 7 |
| < 1k | 7 | 19 % | 10.4 s | 4 |
- The 1.3–1.6k floor was the same at 800 followers and at 5,300: it is Instagram's test audience, and the
  followers add almost nothing to a new reel's reach (they followed for petrol, war-readiness and plumber reels).
- **Retention is the gate, shares the multiplier.** Every pipeline reel with retention ≥ 46 % and real shares broke
  out (002, 026, 030, 008). No reel under 45 % passed 5k views, even with very high share rates: REEL-054 (gas) 160
  shares/1k → 4.7k; REEL-034 (voice clone) 218/1k → 2.4k; REEL-053 145/1k → 2.5k. REEL-010 had 50 % retention but
  8 shares/1k → 2.6k.
- Length is not the problem: Nov reels are 34–47 s and still lose the viewer at 8–15 s.

## What Mirror is (say it this way)
- «Mirror» is a guided AI self-reflection experience: **one conversation, one reflection**. In a single,
  non-repeating session it helps a person see the hidden patterns under their decisions, beliefs and
  identity («سیستم‌عامل درونی‌ات»), and ends with a written reflection report.
- It is **not** therapy, diagnosis, coaching or a chatbot friend; it never claims to remember separate sessions,
  and it does not ask for a name or contact details. It works in Persian.
- The conversation is kept locally on the user's device during the session (a resume code brings it back).

## Never claim (the product's own rules forbid it)
- that Mirror "deletes itself", "stores nothing" or that data disappears when the page closes;
- that it treats, diagnoses or replaces a psychologist; any outcome promise («زندگیت عوض میشه»).

## Where the call to action goes
- **Fit topics** (stress, overthinking, burnout, loneliness, big decisions, career change, «کی هستم؟»،
  relationships, emigration doubts, parenting pressure): about 1 reel in 4 and 1 carousel in 3 end with a Mirror
  bridge: one honest line connecting the topic to self-reflection, then «یه گفتگوی رایگان/یه‌باره با Mirror:
  mirror-pro.com — لینک در بیو». Free only if the site says so at that time; check the site before writing it.
- Caption: the URL as plain text (Instagram captions are not clickable) + «لینک در بیو».
  First comment: the same line with the URL. Stories: a frame with the URL big and readable.
- Never more than one Mirror CTA a day; never on a breaking-news, tragedy or war-safety reel.
- Track: Reels/Carousel row Notes `mirror_cta=yes`; ANALYTICS compares profile visits and link taps on those days.
- The link in the Instagram bio is Mahdi's account setting: the automation never changes it.
